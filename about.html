From the lab bench to atomistic modeling

Physics in Pakistan

I earned my BS in physics at the University of Peshawar in 2017 and my MS in physics at the National University of Sciences and Technology in 2019. Making oxide memory devices and measuring their current–voltage characteristics left me with a question I still ask. What changed inside the material to produce the signal on the screen?

Electrical and computer engineering at UT Austin

I completed my PhD in 2026, advised by Prof. Deji Akinwande and co-mentored by Prof. Wennie Wang. For my dissertation, I used first-principles calculations to investigate atomic-scale switching in two-dimensional memory materials, including MoS₂ and h-BN.

Research tools

Electronic structure and transport

I use density functional theory and quantum transport calculations to relate atomic bonding, charge redistribution, and electron transmission to device behavior.

Atomic pathways and dynamics

I use nudged elastic band calculations to compare migration barriers and ab initio molecular dynamics to follow how atoms move at finite temperature.

Scientific computing

My work uses VASP, Quantum ESPRESSO, QuantumATK, and RMG-DFT. I build analysis workflows in Python and run simulations at TACC, NERSC, and ORNL.
