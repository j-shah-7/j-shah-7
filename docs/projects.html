Metal migration in monolayer MoS₂

First-principles calculations reveal how Au, Ag, and Cu interact with sulfur vacancies and migrate through device-relevant pathways. The energy needed to release an atom from an electrode, move it across MoS₂, and bind it at a sulfur vacancy depends on the metal.

Resistive switching in monolayer h-BN

This work examines vertical and interface-mediated migration toward atomic vacancies in h-BN. It connects vacancy identity, electrode separation, activation barriers, finite-temperature motion, and charge redistribution to the stability of conductive configurations.
