Selected projects

Atomic mechanisms, resolved.

Three material systems, one central question: which microscopic pathway produces the electronic response?

01 · RESISTIVE MEMORY · PUBLISHED

Metal migration in monolayer MoS₂

First-principles calculations compare how Au, Ag and Cu detach from electrodes, diffuse across a monolayer and adsorb at sulfur vacancies. Even in the same host material, the preferred configurations and energetics depend on the metal.

02 · ULTRATHIN DIELECTRICS · ONGOING

Metal diffusion through vacancies in h-BN

This work examines the roles of boron and nitrogen vacancies, metal–layer separation, charge redistribution and finite-temperature dynamics in Au, Ag and Cu migration across hexagonal boron nitride.

03 · PHASE-CHANGE MATERIALS · COLLABORATIVE

Crystalline phases of In₂Se₃

Electronic-structure calculations compare α, β and mixed phase configurations to study how structural changes influence material properties and the electronic density of states, in collaboration with experimental researchers.
