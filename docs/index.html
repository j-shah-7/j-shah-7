My approach

Connecting simulation to experiment.

During my master’s research, I fabricated oxide memory devices and measured their current–voltage response. Today I investigate the atomic rearrangements that may produce those signals, in collaboration with experimental researchers.

My background and tools ↗
01

Define a physical question

Start from a device observation or a specific material functionality.

02

Model the mechanism

Combine DFT, NEB, ab initio dynamics and quantum transport where appropriate.

03

Test predictions

Compare computational trends against experiments and refine the model.

Teaching & mentoring

Start with the question. Build the intuition.

In circuits labs and electronic-device courses, I ask students to explain the physics in their own words, test a prediction and learn from what the instrument shows.
